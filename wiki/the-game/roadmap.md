# Roadmap and funding

Draho is funded one milestone at a time. Each milestone has a goal, a clear result and a "done" test.
All details are on the [funding page](https://sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho/funding/).

## Milestone 1: 4 hours of playable content

A full first chapter: the apartment and garden, new places (the neighbour's flat, a bakery, the rooftops),
a bounty board, smarter mice, coins and save data.

## Milestone 2: Graphical overhaul

Visible fur on Drahomil, and vegetation that moves: grass, ivy, shrubs and trees. Better materials for wood,
stone, roof tiles and brick.

## Milestone 3: Day and night cycle

Dawn, day, dusk and night. Lamps turn on at dusk, mice are bolder at night, and some jobs happen only at night.

## Our promises

* The test build stays free for everyone.
* Each update links to a build you can play.
* No pay-to-win. Money buys looks, never an easier hunt.
* After each milestone, we publish what we spent.
