# About the game

## The idea

You play **Drahomil**, a black house cat. People hire him to hunt mice. He starts at home, in a cozy
apartment with a garden. Later, neighbours, shops and other buildings hire him.

A job goes like this:

1. Accept a job: a client has a mouse problem.
2. Go to the place: a new building or room.
3. Explore and find signs: droppings, noises, holes.
4. Stalk the mouse, then pounce.
5. Get paid in coins and reputation. Knock things over for fun, but some clients do not like that.

## Three pillars

1. **Feel like a real cat.** The movement, the animations and the scale come from a cat's point of view.
   Furniture is big. The gap under the sofa is a hiding place.
2. **Cozy, with bursts of chaos.** Calm rooms, soft light and purring, with short moments of chaos in a hunt.
3. **Short sessions on mobile.** A job takes 3 to 8 minutes. You play with two thumbs and can stop at any time.

## Who it is for

Casual and mid-core mobile players, cat lovers, age 10 and up.

## Based on a real cat

Drahomil, his home, his garden and the animals around him are real. The first level is built from the real
apartment and garden, measured with open map data from Flanders. Read more on the [Drahomil](../characters/drahomil.md) page.
