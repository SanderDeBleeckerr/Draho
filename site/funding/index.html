Help Drahomil own the night.

Drahomil is a black house cat and a mouse bounty hunter. Neighbours, shops and old buildings hire him to clear their mice. We build Draho in the open, and you can play the test build today. This page tells you how we pay for the work and what we promise in return.

Funding strategy

Money follows milestones.

We do not ask for one big sum up front. We split the work into three milestones. Each milestone has a clear goal, a clear result and a clear "done" test. We start a milestone when its money is in, and we show the result in the public test build.

Now

Direct support

Small one-time or monthly gifts from players who follow the project. This money keeps the test build online and pays for assets and tools.

Next

Crowdfunding campaign

One campaign with the three milestones below as stretch goals. Backers get early builds, their name in the credits and a say in the next location.

Next

Public game funds

We will apply to the Flemish game fund (VAF) and look at the Belgian tax shelter for games. Crowdfunding results show these funds that players want the game.

At launch

Fair game revenue

Draho will be free to play with cosmetic purchases: collars, beds, fur patterns. No pay-to-win. This money pays for new locations after launch.

Where each euro goes

  • Development time55%
  • Art, animation and assets20%
  • Sound and cat recordings10%
  • Test devices, build servers, licences10%
  • Campaign fees and rewards5%

Rules we keep

  1. One milestone at a time. We do not spend money for milestone 2 on milestone 1.
  2. Public proof. Every milestone ends in the free test build, so you can check it yourself.
  3. Open books. After each milestone we publish what we spent, per category above.
  4. Scope before delay. If time runs short, we cut extras, not the promise.
Milestones

Three promises, in order.

Each milestone builds on the one before it. First the game, then the look, then the living city.

1

4 hours of playable content

Goal € 12,000 · raised € 0

A full first chapter: enough jobs, places and mice for about four hours of play for a new player.

You get

  • The full apartment and garden, plus new locations: the neighbour's flat, a bakery and the rooftops
  • A bounty board with jobs, clients and bonus goals
  • Mice that hear, hide and come back when it is quiet
  • Coins, reputation and first cosmetics
  • Save and load, so you can stop at any time

How we test it

  • Five new players finish the chapter
  • Their median play time is 4 hours or more
  • It runs on an iPhone 11 at a stable frame rate

Done when: the chapter is in the public test build and passes the play-time test.

2

Graphical overhaul

Goal € 8,000 · raised € 0

Drahomil and his world get the look from the picture above: soft fur, living plants and warm light.

You get

  • Visible fur on Drahomil: layered fur with a sheen and a rim light, so a black cat reads well on screen
  • Fur that moves when he runs, jumps and lands
  • Vegetation: grass, ivy, shrubs and trees that move in the wind and bend when the cat walks through
  • Better materials for wood, stone, roof tiles and brick

How we test it

  • Side-by-side before and after captures for every location
  • It still runs on an iPhone 11 (lower fur and grass detail there)
  • Settings let players pick quality or battery life

Done when: fur and vegetation are in every milestone-1 location in the test build.

3

Day and night cycle

Goal € 6,000 · raised € 0

Time moves. The city changes from morning light to moonlit roofs, and the hunt changes with it.

You get

  • A smooth cycle: dawn, day, dusk and night, with a sun, a moon and stars
  • Lamps, windows and street lights that turn on at dusk
  • Mice are bolder at night; some jobs happen only at night
  • Drahomil's eyes shine in the dark
  • Day and night sounds: birds in the morning, a quiet city at night

How we test it

  • A full cycle in every location without lighting errors
  • Night is dark but always readable on a phone screen
  • No frame-rate drop on an iPhone 11 at dusk

Done when: the cycle runs in every location and at least two night-only jobs are in the test build.

Questions

Good to know.

Where can I play the game now?

Open the test build in Safari or any modern browser. On an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The build changes often, so things can break.

Which platforms will the finished game support?

iPhone first, in landscape. The web build is for testing. Other platforms come after launch, if players ask for them.

Why this order of milestones?

Content first, because a good-looking game with nothing to do is not a game. Then the look, because it touches every location from milestone 1. Then day and night, because it needs the new lighting and plants from milestone 2.

Is the city in the picture real?

The rooftops are inspired by old Flemish cities: towers, stepped gables and canals. Draho uses real measurements from open Flemish map data for its places.
