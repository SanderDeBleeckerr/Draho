# How to play

## Open the test build

1. Open [sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho](https://sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho/) in Safari or another modern browser.
2. On an iPhone, turn the phone sideways (landscape).
3. Optional: tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, so it opens like an app.

The first load can take some time, because the browser downloads the game.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys | Left thumb (floating joystick) |
| Run | Hold Shift | Push the joystick to the edge |
| Jump (keeps the running speed) | Space | Tap the stick (short touch, no push) |
| Stand up on the hind legs (when still) | Space | Tap the stick |
| Climb (in front of ivy or a tree trunk) | Space, then W / S | Hold the middle of the stick, or tap, then push up or down. Tap to leap off |
| Duck and sneak (on/off) | C or Ctrl | **DUCK** button, bottom right |
| Turn the camera | Drag with the right mouse button | Swipe with the right thumb |

## Tips

* Sneak (duck) to get close to blackbirds and mice. A fast fall or a run makes noise.
* Look for things to jump on: the sofa, the table, the cat tree, the picnic table.
