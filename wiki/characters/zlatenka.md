# Zlatenka

**Zlatenka** is a **leopard tortoise** and Drahomil's **childhood friend**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Species** | Leopard tortoise (spotted shell, like a leopard's coat) |
| **Relation to Drahomil** | Childhood friend |

## Her story

When Drahomil was a small kitten, Zlatenka was already there. She is slow, calm and never in a hurry. A young
cat can jump around her, sniff her shell and sleep next to her in the sun, and she does not mind.

The kitten grew up fast. Zlatenka still takes her time. They are still friends.

## Her name

Zlatenka comes from the Czech word for gold, *zlato*. It means "little golden one".
