# The developer

Draho is made by **Sander**, a developer from Flanders, Belgium. Draho is his **hobby project**.

## How it started

On the night of **14 October 2026**, Sander could not sleep. Drahomil was around, as always, and the idea came:
a game where this spoiled black cat is a mouse bounty hunter.

That same night, the first design document, the first Unity project and the first test build went online.
The first level is his own home: the kitchen, the living room and the garden where Drahomil lives.

## Why build in the open

* Everyone can play the test build for free and see the progress.
* The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/SanderDeBleeckerr/Draho).
* The [funding page](https://sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho/funding/) shows the plan and the promises.
