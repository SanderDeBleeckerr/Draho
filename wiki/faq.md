# FAQ

## What is Draho?

Draho is a cozy 3D mobile game about Drahomil, a black cat who hunts mice as a bounty hunter.

## Is Drahomil a real cat?

Yes. Drahomil is a real black cat from Flanders, born in May 2025. Read [his story](characters/drahomil.md).

## Can I play it?

Yes. The test build is free at [sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho](https://sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho/).
It is early, so things can break.

## Which platforms?

iPhone first, in landscape. The web build is for testing.

## When does it come out?

There is no release date yet. Draho started in October 2026 as a hobby project.
See the [roadmap](the-game/roadmap.md).

## Will it cost money?

The plan is free to play with cosmetic purchases only: collars, beds, fur patterns. No pay-to-win.

## Who makes it?

Jim, a solo hobby developer from Belgium. See [The developer](behind-the-game/developer.md).

## How can I help?

Play the test build, follow the project on [GitHub](https://github.com/SanderDeBleeckerr/Draho), and look at the
[funding page](https://sanderdebleeckerr.github.io/Draho/funding/).
