# Fatsocat

**Fatsocat** is Drahomil's **real enemy**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Species** | Cat, white, very big and very fat |
| **Relation to Drahomil** | Enemy and rival for the garden |

## His story

Fatsocat does not live with Drahomil. He **comes into Drahomil's garden** anyway, and then there is a
**fight**. Drahomil is smaller and faster. Fatsocat is bigger and heavier. Neither one gives up the garden.

Zlatenka is a friend and Hrozna is only loud. Fatsocat is the one true threat in Drahomil's world.
