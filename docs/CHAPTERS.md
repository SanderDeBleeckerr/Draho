# Chapters: plan to 4 hours of play

The milestone is about **4 hours of gameplay**. Each chapter is one area of the real block around the
apartment, measured with the `flemish-site-survey` skill (footprints from GRB, heights from the DHM II
surface and terrain models). Only relative game metres go into this repository.

| # | Chapter | Area | Main activities | Target time | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The scratching sound | Apartment and garden | Explore, jump, catch the mouse, stalk blackbirds, climb the maple and the ivy | 25 min | Playable |
| 2 | The Rooftops | Flat roofs behind the ivy wall (3.7–8.6 m) | Climb the ivy to the roof, drainpipe to the high block, drop to the low roof, a drainpipe back up | 30 min | First part playable |
| 3 | Over the Fence | The neighbour's wild garden (nobody home for months), workshop wall | A loose plank in the fence, tall grass, brambles, a hedgehog that must learn to trust the cat, its nest | 30 min | First part playable |
| 4 | The Riverside | Roofs west of the garden, the quay, a narrow edge above the river | Drop to the quay, walk the edge or the rooftops, do not fall in (the cat climbs out wet) | 25 min | First part playable |
| 5 | The Begijnhof | An old walled court of white houses, lindens and a church, down the river | Ivy over the wall, explore the court | 25 min | First part playable |
| 6 | The Bakery | Across a footbridge, on the far bank of the river | Leave the begijnhof over its wall, cross the bridge, catch the bakery mouse | 20 min | First part playable |
| 7 | The Neighbour's Flat | The flat behind the apartment, through a window from the wild garden | Hrozna the corgi sleeps on Drahomil's toy mouse: sneak in, take it back, get out | 20 min | First part playable |
| 8 | The inner court | The round-stone court: a light well in the house (8.8 m) | Pigeons that flee a straight approach (sneak past from the side), a flock that flies up together, a drainpipe to the lost jingle ball | 15 min | First part playable |
| 9 | Night | All areas after sunset | Moths, a fox, the street light; find the way home | 35 min | Planned |
| 10 | The high roofs | Pitched roofs of the street (9–12 m) | Gutters, skylights, chimneys, a storm | 40 min | Planned |
| 11 | Home | Back to the apartment | The humans come home; a gift for them | 15 min | Planned |
|   | Side activities | Everywhere | 24 feathers to find, a bounty board with 5 mouse territories (mice come back warier), coins | 40 min | First part playable |

Rules for each chapter:
- Every new area comes from the real block (survey first, then the blueprint, then the scene).
- Each chapter adds one new cat skill or creature, so play stays fresh.
- A chapter is "done" when the playtest loop (docs/PLAYTEST_LOOP.md) can play it from start to end.
