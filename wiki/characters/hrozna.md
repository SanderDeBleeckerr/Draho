# Hrozna

**Hrozna** is a **Cardigan corgi** with a Czech name. She **sometimes visits** Drahomil.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Species** | Dog, Cardigan corgi |
| **Relation to Drahomil** | Frenemy: loud, but not a real enemy |

## Her story

When Hrozna visits, the house is not quiet. She **barks at Drahomil**, and when he does not watch his bowl,
she **eats his food**. For a spoiled cat, that is a big crime.

But in the end, **they are not really enemies**. After the barking stops, they share the same house and the
same floor. Drahomil keeps his distance, and Hrozna forgets what the fuss was about.

## Her name

*Hrozná* is Czech for "terrible". It is a loving joke for a small dog with a loud voice.
